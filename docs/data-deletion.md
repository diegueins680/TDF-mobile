# TDF Records Mobile Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-03-13

To request deletion of account-linked mobile app data, email `soporte@tdfrecords.com` with the subject line `Mobile Data Deletion Request`.

## Include

- The organization or workspace name.
- The user email tied to the account.
- The platform used: iOS, Android, or both.
- The specific data you want deleted if the request is partial.

## What Happens Next

- TDF Records support will verify the request.
- Access can be revoked to stop further processing.
- Requested deletions will be handled according to operational and legal retention requirements.

For general support, see:

`https://tdf-app.pages.dev/mobile-app/support.html`
