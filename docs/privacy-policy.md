# TDF Records Mobile Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-13

TDF Records operates the TDF Records mobile application for internal business operations and authorized user workflows.

## Information We Process

- Account and profile information needed to authenticate authorized users.
- Operational data created or viewed in the app, including bookings, venues, events, parties, inventory, pipelines, and related notes.
- Uploaded images when a user chooses to attach photos from the camera or photo library.
- Approximate or precise foreground location only when a user grants permission and opens venue discovery features.
- Technical logs needed for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

## How We Use Information

- To provide access to TDF Records operational workflows.
- To sync app activity with TDF backend services.
- To support inventory image capture, venue discovery, and contact card scanning already present in the app.
- To maintain security, diagnose failures, and improve service reliability.

## Sharing

We share information only with service providers and infrastructure required to run TDF Records systems. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention

Information is retained only as long as needed for operational, support, security, and legal purposes.

## Your Choices

- Camera, photo library, and location permissions can be denied or revoked in device settings.
- Authorized organizations can request access, correction, or deletion through TDF Records support.
